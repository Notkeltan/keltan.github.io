Projects

  • No Party Line nopartyline.com

    A dated, sourced public record of people across parties, borders and professions who have warned that advanced AI could escape human control or end us.

  • extinction.meme extinction.meme

    A free pile of AI extinction memes. Scroll them, swipe them, build a pack, share them.

  • Days Since The Last Major AI Company F**kup dayssince.keltan.net

    A daily counter on a workplace-safety sign, reset whenever a frontier AI company had to apologise. It ran for 33 days and the archive stays up.
